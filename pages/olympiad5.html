Ajang Potensi Matematika (APOTEMA) — 2nd Winner

APOTEMA (Ajang Potensi Matematika) is an annual mathematics competition organized by the Mathematics Student Association (HIMATIKA) of UIN Sayyid Ali Rahmatullah Tulungagung. The event brings together students from various educational levels to compete in olympiad-style mathematics while also promoting scientific writing through a national essay competition. APOTEMA emphasizes logical reasoning, analytical thinking, and advanced problem-solving skills in mathematics, serving as a platform to foster mathematical excellence and academic achievement.
