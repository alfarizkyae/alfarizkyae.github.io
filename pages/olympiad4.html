Olimpiade Matematika Vektor Nasional (OMVN) — 1st Winner

Olimpiade Matematika Vektor Nasional (OMVN) is a prestigious national mathematics competition organized by HIMATIKA Vektor, the Mathematics Student Association of Universitas Negeri Malang (UM). The competition brings together elementary, junior high, and senior high school students from across Indonesia to solve challenging olympiad-level problems in algebra, geometry, combinatorics, and number theory. OMVN aims to cultivate critical thinking, logical reasoning, mathematical creativity, and a passion for mathematics through a rigorous multi-round competition.
