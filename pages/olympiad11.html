Mathematics Competition Revolution (MCR) — 3rd Runner up

Mathematics Competition Revolution (MCR) is a national mathematics competition organized by the Mathematics Student Associations of the Department of Mathematics at Universitas Negeri Surabaya (UNESA). The competition brings together students from elementary, junior high, and senior high schools across Indonesia to solve challenging olympiad-style mathematics problems. MCR emphasizes logical reasoning, analytical thinking, and mathematical creativity through multiple rounds of competition, including online preliminary rounds and offline semifinal and final stages.
