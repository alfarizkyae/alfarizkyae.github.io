Kompetisi Matematika (KOMET) — 1st Winner

KOMET (Kompetisi Matematika) is a national mathematics olympiad organized by the Mathematics Student Association (HMPS "Integral") of the Mathematics Study Program at UIN Maulana Malik Ibrahim Malang. The competition challenges high school students with olympiad-level mathematics problems designed to assess analytical thinking, logical reasoning, and advanced problem-solving skills in areas such as algebra, geometry, combinatorics, and number theory. KOMET also serves to foster mathematical excellence and promote interest in higher mathematics among students nationwide.
