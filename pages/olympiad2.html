International Gamma Mathematics Olympiad — Bronze Medalist

International Gamma Math Olympiad (IGMO) is a global mathematics competition that brings together talented students and mathematics enthusiasts from around the world. The Olympiad emphasizes advanced problem-solving, logical reasoning, and mathematical creativity through challenging proof-based and olympiad-style problems across areas such as algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory. Participants compete internationally and are recognized based on their mathematical performance and analytical skills.
